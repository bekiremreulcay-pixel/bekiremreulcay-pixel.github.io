FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will my draft arrive?

+

Within 24-48 hours of sending us the name, on WhatsApp, inshallah. The draft is free; if you don't like it, there is no obligation.

Is the calligraphy really handwritten?

+

Yes. Every name is written by hand by our third-generation calligrapher in Ottoman style; the piece is then crafted from that exact calligraphy.

What are the prices?

+

Bank transfer prices: Zarif series from 3,900 ₺, Saltanat series from 5,500 ₺, each extra word +1,000 ₺, gold plating +1,000 ₺. Single letter 990 ₺, intertwined double letter 1,900 ₺. Solid gold: 8K from 24,000 ₺, 14K from 42,000 ₺, 18K from 56,000 ₺. Card single payment adds 25%.

How long does delivery take?

+

Draft within 24-48 hours, crafting within 10-12 business days after approval, inshallah, then shipping with Aras Kargo. Shipping within Türkiye is free.

Which alphabet is used?

+

Your name can be written in Turkish letters in Ottoman calligraphy style, or in the Arabic alphabet. Our calligrapher writes every name specially for you; like a fingerprint, each writing is unique.

Is sizing needed?

+

No piece needs a size. Rings are open-backed and adjustable; bracelet chains are adjustable.

Why is the card price different from the bank transfer price?

+

The difference covers only bank commissions and card transaction/service fees.

Does plating fade?

+

Plated pieces come with a lifetime anti-tarnish guarantee.

What comes in the box?

+

Silver pieces come in a velvet box, gold pieces in a mahogany box, with the original ink calligraphy of your name and a signed certificate of authenticity.
